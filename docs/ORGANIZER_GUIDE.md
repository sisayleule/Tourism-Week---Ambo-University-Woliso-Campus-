## 1. Sign in and change your password

1. Go to `/staff/login`.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. If this is your first login, you are asked to set a new password right away. It must be at least **12 characters** — use a mix of letters, numbers, and a symbol.
4. After setting your password you land on the Dashboard.

**Forgot your password?** Click "Forgot your password?" on the login page. If the department does not have email configured, ask a colleague to reset it for you using the SQL snippet in `docs/TEAM_ACCOUNTS.md`.

**Session timeout:** You are signed out automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity. A warning appears at 25 minutes — click "Stay signed in" to continue.

## 2. Set up a new edition (This Year's Event)

The **This Year's Event** wizard walks you through each step in order. Open it from the sidebar under **EVENT → This Year's Event**.

**Steps inside the wizard:**
1. **Basic information** — edition number, title in English/Amharic/Afaan Oromo, theme, venue, start and end dates.
2. **Days** — confirm or add named days (Day 1, Day 2, …).
3. **Committee** — add committee members who appear on the About page.
4. **Review** — a summary before you save.

The wizard saves each step as you go. You can leave and come back.

**To create a new edition from a previous one:**  
Go to **EVENT → Editions**, click **Create new edition**, choose the source edition, and the wizard copies all events from that edition as drafts. Edit and publish them before setting the new edition as live.

## 3. Add and edit events (Program)

Go to **EVENT → Program** in the sidebar.

**To add an event:**
1. Click **Add event**.
2. Fill in: title (English required, Amharic and Afaan Oromo optional), day, session (morning or afternoon), category, venue, start and end time, description.
3. Upload a cover image (optional).
4. Click **Save as draft** to save without showing it on the public site.
5. When ready, click **Publish** to make it visible.

**To edit an event:** click its title in the list.

**To delete an event:** click the event, then click **Delete** at the bottom. A confirmation dialog appears. You will be asked to confirm your password — this is a safety check.

**Program view:** switch between "List" and "By day" tabs at the top of the Program page to see events in a table or arranged by day.

---

## 4. Run the event live (Live Control)

Go to **EVENT → Live Control**.

Each event has a status: **Scheduled**, **Live**, **Delayed**, **Cancelled**, or **Finished**.

1. Click an event's status button to change it.
2. Add an optional status note in English/Amharic/Afaan Oromo — this appears on the public site next to the event.
3. Changes take effect on the public site after the next website update (usually a few seconds to a few minutes).

The **Update website now** button in the sync bar at the top of every page forces an immediate update if you need changes visible right away.

---

## 5. Post an announcement

Go to **CONTENT → Announcements**.

1. Click **New announcement**.
2. Write the message in English (Amharic and Afaan Oromo optional), choose a type (Normal, Important, or Urgent), and set a start date/time.
3. Set an optional expiry date — the announcement disappears from the public site after that time.
4. Click **Publish** to make it live, or **Save as draft** to review later.

To delete an announcement: open it and click **Delete**. A confirmation dialog appears.

---

## 6. Post a news article

Go to **CONTENT → News**.

1. Click **New article**.
2. Add a title, body text, and an optional cover image.
3. Enter a short summary for the article list page.
4. Click **Publish** to make it visible, or **Save as draft**.

To delete an article: open it and click **Delete**. Confirm in the dialog and confirm your password.

---

## 7. Upload photos (Gallery)

Go to **CONTENT → Gallery**.

1. Click **Add photo**.
2. Upload an image. The portal resizes it automatically.
3. Add a caption in English (optional) and an alt text for accessibility.
4. Click **Publish** to show it on the public gallery.

To delete a photo: open it and click **Delete**.

---

## 8. Manage sponsors

Go to **CONTENT → Sponsors**.

1. Click **Add sponsor**.
2. Enter the name, tier (Gold, Silver, Bronze, Partner, Community), and upload a logo.
3. Add a website link (optional).
4. Click **Publish**.

Drag sponsors to reorder them within their tier. Changes are saved automatically.

---

## 9. Manage committee members

Go to **CONTENT → Committee**.

1. Click **Add member**.
2. Enter name, role/title, and an optional photo.
3. Add contact details (email or phone) if you want them shown publicly — only shown when the contact's "Show public" toggle is on.
4. Click **Publish**.

---

## 10. Update website text (Website Text)

Go to **CONTENT → Website Text**.

This page lists every editable section of the public website, grouped by page (Home, About, Contact, etc.).

1. Click **Edit** on any section.
2. Update the text in English (Amharic and Afaan Oromo optional).
3. Upload a background image or card image if the section has one.
4. Click **Save** (saves as draft) or **Publish** to make it live.

After saving, the **Update website now** button in the sync bar pushes the changes to the public site.

---

## 11. Read and handle messages

Go to **INBOX & SETTINGS → Messages**. The unread count badge in the sidebar shows how many messages are waiting.

Messages come from the Contact page and sponsor inquiry form on the public website.

- Click a message to expand it and read the full text.
- Click **Mark as handled** when you have replied or taken action.
- Click **Delete** to remove a message permanently. This requires password confirmation.

**Privacy:** Messages contain visitors' private contact details. Do not share them outside the department.

---

## 12. Update contact information and social links

Go to **INBOX & SETTINGS → Website Settings**, then the **Contact & Links** tab.

Update:
- Contact email and phone number (leave phone empty to hide it on the public site).
- Address in each language you use.
- Social media links — each must start with `https://`. Leave empty to hide the icon.
- Footer text shown at the bottom of every public page.

Click **Save settings**. You will be asked to confirm your password, then the changes are queued for the next website update.

---

## 13. Download a backup

Go to **INBOX & SETTINGS → Website Settings**, then the **Backup** tab.

1. Click **Download backup**.
2. Confirm your password when prompted.
3. A ZIP file downloads to your computer with all content tables as JSON, one CSV per table, and a list of every image URL.

**Store it safely** — the backup includes private visitor messages. Save it in the department's restricted Google Drive folder, not in a shared folder.

A reminder banner appears if no backup has been taken in the last 30 days.

---

## 14. View change history and restore a previous version

Go to **INBOX & SETTINGS → Website Settings**, then the **History** tab.

The table shows every change made in the portal — who made it, what they changed, and when.

**To see what changed in a row:** click the row. A panel opens showing the before and after values side by side.

**To restore a previous version:**
1. Click **Restore this version** on a row that shows "Added" or "Changed".
2. Confirm in the dialog — the item is restored to what it was before that change.
3. Confirm your password.

What "restore" does:
- **Changed** → reverts the item to its previous state.
- **Deleted** → recreates the deleted item as it was.

---

## 15. Publish a new edition as the live edition

Go to **EVENT → Editions**.

1. Find the edition you want to make live. Make sure it is **published** (not draft) and has published events.
2. Click **Set as current**.
3. Confirm in the dialog and confirm your password.
4. The sync bar shows "Changes pending". Click **Update website now** to push the new edition to the public site.

**Warning:** if the edition has no published events yet, the public schedule will be empty.

## 16. Yearly checklist

At the start of each new Tourism Week cycle, work through these steps:

| Step | Task |
|------|------|
| 1 | Log in to [supabase.com](https://supabase.com) and resume the project if it is paused (free plan pauses after one week of no connections). |
| 2 | Create the new edition using **This Year's Event** or **Editions → Create new edition**. |
| 3 | Update the committee and site settings for the new year. |
| 4 | Edit the copied draft events and publish them. |
| 5 | During the event, use **Live Control** to mark events live, delayed, or cancelled. |
| 6 | After the event, download a backup from **Website Settings → Backup**. |
| 7 | Run `npm run snapshot:save` and commit `content/snapshot.last-good.json` to the repository. |
| 8 | Confirm that GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, and the domain are all registered to department email addresses (not personal ones). |

## 17. Security tips

- **Use a strong password** — at least 12 characters, mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
- **Never share your password** — each person should have their own account. See `docs/TEAM_ACCOUNTS.md` to request a new account.
- **Lock your screen** when you leave your desk. The portal signs you out after 30 minutes of inactivity.
- **Sensitive actions** (delete, backup, settings save, restoring a version, setting the current edition) ask you to confirm your password. This is an extra safety step — it is normal.
- **Temporary passwords** must be sent privately — Telegram or WhatsApp direct message, never in a group chat.
- The portal is excluded from search engines (`/staff` has `noindex` headers and is in `robots.txt`).
